# Human Physiology for Engineers

Quantitative physiology through computational models: cell population dynamics, diffusion and mass action, membrane potentials, enzyme kinetics, Hodgkin-Huxley action potentials, systemic blood pressure, left-heart mechanics, and glucose-insulin regulation.

## Open the visualizations

**[Launch the visualized homework examples](https://jonnyterrero.github.io/BME-Computations-and-Visualizations/human-physiology-for-engineers/visualized-examples/index.html)**

Or open [`visualized-examples/index.html`](visualized-examples/index.html) locally in a browser.

| --- | --- |
