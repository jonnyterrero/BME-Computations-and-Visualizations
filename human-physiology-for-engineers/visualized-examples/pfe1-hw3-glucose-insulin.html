Homework 3 — Minimal Glucose-Insulin Model (GLUINSMODEL)

Interactive version of Dr. Khoo’s minimal insulin/glucose model (Roth/Lieber modifications). Glucose balance: CG dG/dt = QL + Qmeal(t) − λG − η·νGI − μ(G−θ)+; insulin balance: CI dI/dt = β·κ(G−φ)+ − αI. κ is the Type 1 severity gain (insulin production) and η the Type 2 severity gain (insulin sensitivity).  ← back to index

Scenario presets (from the assignment)

Normal fasting adult: gains = 1, QL = 8400 mg/hr, no glucose load. Steady state ≈ 0.81 mg/ml glucose, 0.057 IU/ml insulin.

OGTT positivity criteria used in the assignment: glucose ≥ 2.0 mg/ml at 1 hr or ≥ 1.4 mg/ml at 2 hr after the 75 g load. Fasting levels are found automatically by pre-running the model to steady state before the test begins (the load is given at t = 0.5 hr).

Pharmacokinetics used (one-compartment oral/subcutaneous models)

Metformin: dose 1 g, Vd = 15 L, ka = 0.275/hr, ke = 0.25/hr, F = 0.95. Bateman concentration C(t) = F·D·ka/[Vd(ka−ke)]·(e−ket−e−kat). Hepatic output is scaled as QL(t) = QL0·[1 − 0.5·C(t)/Cmax,1g].
Humulin: Vd = 30 L, ka = 1.55/hr, ke = 0.43/hr, F = 0.65 (peak at ≈1.1 hr, duration ≈5 hr, matching the Lilly data quoted in the assignment). With the course units a 30 IU dose produces a small plasma concentration relative to the model’s fasting insulin of 0.057 IU/ml — raise the dose slider to explore a clear hypoglycemic nadir.
